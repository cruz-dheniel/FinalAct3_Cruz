Password visibility icons
=========================

These transparent 24 x 24 PNGs were resized from the user's supplied
Restaurant_WinForms_Icon_Pack, using the red icons for white panels:

EyeOpen.png: 02_RED_for_White_Panel/64px/02_Login_Register/show_password.png
EyeOff.png:  02_RED_for_White_Panel/64px/02_Login_Register/hide_password.png

They are embedded through Properties/Resources.resx. The application does
not load files from Downloads or require loose PNGs after publishing.
